We use radio surveys and spatially resolved VLBA observations to estimate the minimum energies and magnetic field strengths of a subset of edge-brightened CSOs (CSO-2s). These are necessary to test models of CSO-2 formation via stellar capture and evolution via synchrotron cooling.
