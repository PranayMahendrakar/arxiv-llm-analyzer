Abstract

End-to-end physical-layer learning replaces separate modulation, coding, and detection operations with an autoencoder (AE) that consists of a jointly trained encoder-decoder pair at the transmitter and the receiver. This paper studies AI-native communications with AEs and quantifies the sensitivity to training-test mismatch in channel models. Models trained only on an AWGN channel achieve low bit error rates (BERs) under matched conditions but degrade severely on Rayleigh fading with practical front-end impairments. It enables efficient post-training with no architectural changes and no new videos, using only individual images.
