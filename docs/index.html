While current generative methods produce realistic albedo maps, they still struggle to infer accurate metallic and roughness attributes for relighting. We present UltraTex, a cascaded multi-view framework that moves beyond appearance matching by integrating physical knowledge into multi-view diffusion-based PBR generation. Published: 2026-11-18 | Authors: Anto I. Lonappan, Adrian E. Bayer, Brian Keating, Kam Arnold, Giuseppe Puglisi | Tags: astro-ph.CO, astro-ph.IM

Abstract

We train a spherical graph convolutional network to infer the tensor-to-scalar ratio $r$ directly from multifrequency cosmic microwave background (CMB) $B$-mode maps on the cut sky. We test the network on simulations of a generic ground-based experiment including lensed CMB, inhomogeneous noise, and stochastic Galactic dust and synchrotron emission. We compare the network with a multifrequency bandpower likelihood built from the same 20,000 simulations used to train it. Working directly on the maps, with no power-spectrum estimation or component separation, the network reduces the root-mean-square error on $r$ by a factor of 1.3 and gives well-calibrated 68% intervals 1.2 times narrower than the likelihood with the same prior. Abstract

We present LEMUR, a publicly accessible archive of uniformly reduced, deep Chandra images of 2,569 galaxies and galaxy clusters (including groups), comprising 5,505 ACIS observations and 138.8 Ms of exposure. Our primary goal is to make the wealth of archival Chandra observations accessible to researchers without requiring specialized expertise in X-ray data reduction.
