Abstract

For any fixed $k$, we prove a lower bound on the $k$th largest modulus of an eigenvalue of the non-backtracking matrix $B$. Specifically, consider any deterministic or random family of graphs that converges locally to the unimodular Galton-Watson tree with root degree distribution $D$, and set $κ:=\mathbb E[D(D-1)]/\mathbb E[D]$.
